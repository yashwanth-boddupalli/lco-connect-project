FAQ

Common questions

How long does verification take?+

Most LCO registrations are reviewed within 1–2 business days once your documents are submitted.

Can I choose which services to offer?+

Yes. Your dashboard adapts to show only cable, broadband, Wi-Fi, or other services you select during registration.

How do customers get added?+

You create the customer record, and they receive an invitation to set up their own login and verify their phone number.

What payment methods are supported?+

UPI, cards and net banking through our payment gateway, with UPI QR available as a fallback.

Is there a free trial?+

Registration is completely free. Your ₹999/month subscription only begins after your LCO is verified and approved by our team.

Can I manage multiple service types?+

Absolutely. If you provide cable TV, broadband and local Wi-Fi, you can manage all three from a single dashboard with unified billing.
